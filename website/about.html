Quality system

Three-stage inspection on every unit.

Zero-defect is the standard. Every CHINYOO product passes through three quality gates before it leaves our factory.

01

IQC — Incoming Quality Control

Every batch of raw materials is tested before entering production. PCB assemblies, ABS housings, electronic components — all checked against spec.

02

IPQC — In-Process Quality Control

Quality engineers sample-test units at every critical stage: assembly, soldering, calibration, and final assembly.

03

FQC — Final Quality Control

Every single unit is tested for voltage detection accuracy, alarm function, and physical finish before packaging. No batch sampling — 100% inspection.
