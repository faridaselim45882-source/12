Frequently asked

Questions answered.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +
CHINYOO's MOQ is typically 3,000 pieces per model. Sample orders are accepted in any quantity. Mixed-model container orders are welcome, allowing distributors to combine multiple SKUs in one shipment.
Do you offer OEM/ODM and private label services? +
Yes. We provide full OEM/ODM services including custom logo printing, color customization, retail packaging design, and private label manufacturing. Most OEM orders ship within 35-40 days after artwork approval.
Which international certifications do your products carry? +
All CHINYOO voltage testers and multimeters carry CE (EU), FCC (US), and RoHS (lead-free) certifications. CE detection number: SHEM110100002901. We also hold 8 product patents and are SGS tested.
What is the typical sample delivery time? +
Samples are typically delivered within 3 business days from artwork approval, shipped via DHL or FedEx. Sample cost is refunded against your first bulk order.
Can you ship directly to Amazon FBA warehouses? +
Yes. CHINYOO regularly ships to Amazon FBA warehouses in the US, EU, and Japan. We provide DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) terms and handle all compliance labeling for FBA requirements.
